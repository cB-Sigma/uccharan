uccharan transliteration packs (xlit-<language>.ucd)
====================================================

The packs were built by the uccharan project (https://github.com/cB-Sigma/uccharan)

  Dakshina dataset v1.0, word lexicons
  Brian Roark, Lawrence Wolf-Sonkin, Christo Kirov, Sabrina J. Mielke, Cibu

  https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4bharat/Aksharantar
  CC BY (manually collected data) and CC0 (mined data and existing sources)

  Leipzig Corpora Collection (word and word-pair counts)
  D. Goldhahn, T. Eckart and U. Quasthoff, Leipzig University
  https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/download
  CC BY 4.0; the Wikipedia samples carry text of Wikipedia contributors,
  CC BY-SA 4.0

  Tatoeba (Hindi and English word counts)
  https://tatoeba.org, Tatoeba contributors
  CC BY 2.0 FR

and from hand-written lists of the uccharan project (Apache-2.0).

Changes made: the word pairs were aligned and reduced to n-gram statistics
and a lexicon of spellings; the corpora were reduced to word and word-pair
counts.
