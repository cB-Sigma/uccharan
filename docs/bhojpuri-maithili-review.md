# Bhojpuri, Maithili and Magahi: items for a native speaker to check

uccharan's improved mode for Bhojpuri follows published grammars (Shukla 1981, *Bhojpuri Grammar*;
Tiwari 1960; Grierson's *Linguistic Survey of India* V.2, 1903), and its gold set was written from
the same descriptions, so agreement with the gold set does not prove that speakers say it this way.
These are the choices a native speaker should confirm or correct (expected readings in broad IPA):

1. **Past forms in -ल-**: रहलन ɾəhələn, कहलस kəhələs, देखलस deːkʰələs — or with the vowel dropped
   (ɾəhlən, kəhləs), as Hindi-style deletion would give? Same for 1st-person रहनी ɾəhəniː.
2. **निकलल** nikələl or nikləl? **पोखरा** poːkʰəɾaː or poːkʰɾaː? **अइसने** əisəneː or əisneː?
3. **Vowel sequences**: is गइल two syllables (ɡə.il) and कइसन three (kə.i.sən), or a diphthong
   (ɡəil, kəisən as in Hindi-influenced speech)? Same for रउआ ɾə.u.aː.
4. **ऐ औ** in learned loans (वैज्ञानिक, औपचारिक, शैली): əi əu as in native words, or Hindi ɛː ɔː?
5. **Avagraha**: चलऽ cələ, देखऽ deːkʰə — is the final vowel ə, or closer to ɔ/o in your speech?
   Is हs / सs (Latin s typed for ऽ, common online) always this vowel?
6. **Nasal vowels**: गाँव ɡãːʋ — ʋ or w at the end? साँझ sãːɟʰ, चाँद cãːd.
7. **Final ी ू**: बानी baːniː, मेहरारू meːhəɾaːɾuː — long or short in running speech?
8. **Numbers**: दू, छव (or छः / छौ), एगारह, ordinals पहिला, दूसर, तीसर, चउथा; is नौ nəu?
9. **Stress**: improved mode leaves the copula (बा, बानी, बाड़े, हवे), pronouns (हम, ऊ, ई) and
   postpositions unstressed; other words take Hindi-type stress. Is there a word class this is wrong for?
10. **Kaithi**: the letters DDDHA (U+1109A) and RHA (U+1109C) are read as ड़ and ढ़.
11. **Maithili** (first version): final short ि ु are kept (अछि əcʰɪ), ऐ औ as əi əu; number words
    दू, एगारह, पहिल, दोसर, तेसर, चारिम. Which of these are right, and what else differs most from
    Hindi in your reading?
12. **Magahi** (first version, from Verma 2003, Aryani 1965 and Grierson): श ष read s and ण read
    n (देश deːs, कारण kaːɾən) — is that your everyday reading of Sanskrit loans, or ʃ and ɳ?
    Sanskrit loans starting with व read b (विशेष biseːs, व्यवस्था bjəwəstʰaː) — always?
13. **Magahi verb endings**: हलई hələi, गेलय ɡeːləi, होतय hoːtəi, करई kəɾəi as one diphthong
    əi — or two syllables (hə.lə.i), or a long iː (hələiː)? Is कहलकय kəhləkəi or kəhələkəi?
14. **Magahi final ि ु**: अखनि əkʰni, जखनि ɟəkʰni, आगि aːɡi, राति ɾaːti — is the final i said?
15. **Magahi** में read as मे meː (no nasal); हियो hijoː, हको həkoː, हथ hətʰ as unstressed
    auxiliaries; ं after a long vowel as a nasal vowel (जांच ɟãːc) but n before s (फ्रांसीसी).
16. **Magahi** compounds and reduplication: लसलस ləsləs, नौकरी nəukɾiː or nəukəɾiː, निकलल
    nikləl or nikələl, एंहे ẽːheː or eːnheː.
